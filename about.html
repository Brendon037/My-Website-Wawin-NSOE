Our story

About Wawin National School of Excellence

We are a school built on a passion for educational excellence, strong values, and the belief that every learner deserves the opportunity to thrive.

Our history

Wawin National School of Excellence was established with a mission to provide quality education in a supportive and disciplined environment. From its early beginnings, the school has grown into a respected learning community in Lae, serving families who value academic excellence, leadership, and service.

Over the years, the school has built a reputation for nurturing students who are equipped to succeed in school and contribute positively to society.

Vision

To be a leading school that develops capable, responsible, and innovative learners who are ready to meet the challenges of the future with confidence and integrity.

Mission

To deliver quality education through dynamic teaching, strong values, and a nurturing environment that recognizes every learner's potential.

Values

What guides us

Academic excellence

We set high standards and encourage students to pursue success through discipline and dedication.

Respect

We promote mutual respect, fairness, and a culture where every voice matters.

Integrity

We teach honesty, accountability, and responsibility in actions and decisions.

Community service

We believe students should be active, compassionate contributors to their schools and communities.

Achievements

  • Strong academic performance and student progression across key learning areas.
  • Development of leadership qualities through school activities and student representation.
  • Consistent focus on quality teaching, discipline, and positive school culture.
  • Community engagement that reinforces partnership between school, families, and local stakeholders.
